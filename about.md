# About

Hi, I'm Joe — a photographer based in Gainesville, FL specializing in graduation and portrait sessions at the University of Florida.

I focus on clean, campus-focused work that captures the moment without overproducing it. My sessions are low-pressure, simply priced, and designed to fit around your schedule during one of the busiest weeks of your college career.

My work has been selected for licensing by [Minted](https://www.minted.com/), a curated marketplace for independent artists and designers.

## Equipment

- Canon EOS R50 (RF Mount)
- RF 50mm F1.8 STM — primary
- RF 55–210mm F5–7.1 IS STM — travel / backup
- Pocket Dispo — niche / film look
